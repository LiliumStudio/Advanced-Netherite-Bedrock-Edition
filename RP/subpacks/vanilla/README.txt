Vanilla look (default).

This subpack is intentionally empty. Selecting it means no Actions & Stuff attachables
are loaded, so netherite tools and weapons render with the default flat item sprite.

The Actions & Stuff attachables live in the "actions_and_stuff" subpack and are only
loaded when the player selects the Actions & Stuff compatibility option in the pack
settings. They reference the models, animations and render controllers shipped by
Actions & Stuff itself, so this addon ships no tool or weapon geometry of its own.

Armor attachables are part of the base pack and are always loaded. They use the vanilla
armor geometry, which Actions & Stuff does not replace, so armor needs no compatibility
subpack.
